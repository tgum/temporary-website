quiche recipe

tasty type of pie

Ingredients:

  • 1 pâte brisée
  • 400ml cream
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g grated cheese
  • 200g lardons (or ham or vegetables or anything really)
  • salt and pepper

Instructions:

  1. preheat the oven around 180°c
  2. mix all the eggs in a bowl
  3. add the cream and mix pretty evenly
  4. then you can add all the meat/vegetables and half the cheese
  5. put the dough in a tart mold or anything appropriate
  6. pour the mixture in the dough
  7. bake for around 40 minutes
